Our Story

This Writers Coalition began as another one. the founder in the other Writers Coalition (Writers Coalition #2), Aokia, decided to kick Oz out of the Coalition #2 for no good reason. Oz decided to make his own Writers Coalition (Writers Coalition #1) and invite new members.

Though, quickly, Writers Coalition #2 came back and started stealing members from Oz. That is why, to this day, Writers Coalition #1 tries to make better stories.

Competition

The Writers Coalition #2's members are Teemo, Aokai, and Imannuel. Teemo and Aokai are originals, but Imannuel is a stolen member from Writers Coalition #1.

Writers Coalition #1 and Writers Coalition #2 are rivals. They each try to make better books than the other.
