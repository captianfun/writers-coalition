⚡ Origin of the Coalition

The Writers Coalition started as just three kids who liked to write books. They eventually decided to join to make books and collaborate.

Thus, the The Writers Coalition was born!

Eventually, though, two of the members quit, Leaving only Oz. So he decided to make his own Writers Coalition.

He got four members to join him, and they all collaborate to this day!
